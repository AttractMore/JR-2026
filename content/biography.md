Jackie Raw, BA Hons, RS Hom

Jackie is a Homeopath in general practice, with a specialist interest in Rheumatology & Gastroenterology. She qualified as a Homeopath from London College of Classical Homeopathy in 2002 & has subsequently gained accreditation in Post Graduate Rheumatology : Disease Processes, Treatment & Care in 2011 from University of Leeds School of Healthcare.

She believes in a whole-person approach to well-being & health, combining the historical importance of Homeopathy – a system of medicine over 200 years old - with conventional clinical treatment, where needed, to provide the most complete care for the patient.

Her research interests include 4 studies in the Homeopathic Treatment of Fibromyalgia Syndrome (FMS) & Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) & she continues to collaborate & work closely with conventional colleagues in these fields.

She is passionate about supporting each patient in managing & resolving their condition(s) & becoming healthy & restored after injury, illness &/or emotional hurt. Her experience brings a wide knowledge & understanding of other measures which patients can employ to empower their recovery & create positive change.

Outside of Homeopathy Jackie is a lifelong dog person & has rescued & rehabilitated German Shepherd Dogs for 30 years 🐺
